Mission & Fiduciary Commitment

Our sacred mandate to active firefighters, retirees, surviving beneficiaries, and the Laredo community.

OFFICIAL MISSION STATEMENT
"To play an integral role in the future financial security of plan participants by promptly and courteously delivering quality benefits and information which members value and trust through professional plan administration and prudent management of System assets."

— Board of Trustees, Laredo Firefighters Retirement System

Our Core Governance Pillars

The foundational principles that guide every decision of our Board of Trustees and Administrative Staff.

Fiduciary Integrity

Enforcing statutory compliance under the Texas Local Fire Fighters Retirement Act (TLFFRA) and preserving system capitalization for generations of Laredo firefighters.

Prudent Asset Management

Executing disciplined investment strategies reviewed by independent actuaries and investment advisors to ensure long-term fund solvency.

Public Transparency

Providing full public disclosure of all financial audits, meeting agendas, minutes, and Texas Pension Review Board (PRB) statutory reports.

Board Leadership & Fiduciary Governance

Under the leadership of Board Chairman Louis Vaillancourt, Vice Chairman Alfredo Robledo, Firefighter Representatives, and City Representatives, the Board of Trustees maintains active oversight over every dollar invested and every benefit disbursed.
